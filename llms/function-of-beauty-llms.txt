# Function of Beauty

> Function of Beauty is a direct-to-consumer personalization company in the beauty and personal-care space, best known for customizable hair care (shampoo, conditioner) and skin/body products formulated to a quiz-driven profile. The storefront runs on Shopify; its customer-facing programmatic surface is the Shopify Customer Account API (OpenID Connect). Backed by GGV Capital. This profile is maintained by the API Evangelist network.
